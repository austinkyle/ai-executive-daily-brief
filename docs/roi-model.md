# ROI Model

## Purpose and use
This is an **editable, assumptions-based** model for framing potential value during a consulting engagement. All defaults are illustrative: replace them with a client's payroll costs, operating baselines, incident history, and agreed measurement period.

This model does **not** guarantee revenue, savings, loss avoidance, or any other outcome. It makes assumptions visible and testable.

## Editable assumptions table

| Value category | Assumption name | Example default value | Source / rationale | Resulting estimated monthly value or hours |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| Executive reporting hours saved | Executive reporting time avoided | 5 hours/month × $175 fully loaded hourly cost | Founder/executive estimate of time spent assembling and reconciling reporting. | **$875/month** |
| Analyst/operations hours saved | Analyst and operations reporting time avoided | 16 hours/month × $65 fully loaded hourly cost | Time study of exports, refreshes, investigation handoffs, and reporting. | **$1,040/month** |
| Faster detection of revenue problems | Daily revenue at risk; days faster detected; incidents/month | $12,000/day × 1.5 days × 1 incident/month | Conservative incident review of affected daily revenue and detection-time improvement. | **$18,000/month exposure identified earlier** |
| Reduced stockout exposure | SKU daily gross margin; avoidable stockout days; mitigation rate | $2,500/day × 2 days × 25% | Top-SKU sell-through, margin, historical stockout duration, and realistically avoidable portion. | **$1,250/month potential exposure reduction** |
| Reduced wasted ad spend | Monthly paid spend; inefficient-share reduction | $80,000/month × 2% | Channel spend and conservatively supportable reduction from earlier campaign detection/reallocation. | **$1,600/month potential spend redeployment** |
| Improved decision speed | Decisions accelerated; hours saved per decision | 8 decisions/month × 0.5 hour | Recurring campaign, inventory, and support decisions; report capacity unless financial conversion is validated. | **4 hours/month** |

## Formula definitions
- **Executive reporting value** = reporting hours saved/month × fully loaded executive hourly cost.
- **Analyst/operations value** = hours saved/month × fully loaded hourly cost.
- **Faster detection exposure** = average daily revenue at risk × days faster detected × qualifying incidents/month. This is exposure identified earlier, not booked revenue or guaranteed loss avoidance. For an avoided-exposure estimate, use `exposure × client-agreed mitigation rate`.
- **Reduced stockout exposure** = at-risk SKU daily gross margin × avoidable stockout days/month × mitigation rate.
- **Reduced wasted ad spend** = monthly paid spend × conservatively supportable inefficient-share reduction.
- **Improved decision speed** = decisions accelerated/month × hours saved/decision. Convert to dollars only with a client-agreed hourly cost and validated relationship.

## Suggested engagement worksheet
1. Baseline reporting work for two to four weeks: people, hours, tools, and delays.
2. Record real late-detected revenue, inventory, marketing, and support incidents.
3. Agree on materiality, revenue/margin at risk, and conservative mitigation rates.
4. Replace each default, keep its source, and distinguish capacity savings from financial exposure.
5. Revisit after a shadow-validation period using observed brief usage and documented decisions.

Do not sum exposure figures into a promised ROI without client validation and explicit overlap treatment. A stockout, for example, can affect revenue exposure and decision time at once.
